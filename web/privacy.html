Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Standing is a macOS menu bar app that helps you notice when you've been sitting or standing too long. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

What Standing uses

  • Camera — short still frames from your Mac's camera to decide sit vs stand. Frames are processed locally for motion gating; when a posture check is worthwhile, a small JPEG may be sent to TypeSafe's Jev image API using your API key.
  • API key — your TypeSafe key is stored only on your Mac (Application Support / Keychain depending on build). Standing does not receive or store your key on a Standing server.
  • Local stats — sit/stand times, calibration images, and logs stay on your Mac under Application Support and Logs (or the App Sandbox container for Mac App Store builds).

What we don't do

  • No Standing account, no cloud sync of your posture history.
  • No advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no sale of personal data.
  • No continuous video upload — checks run on motion or a slow heartbeat.

Third parties

Posture classification uses TypeSafe Jev when you supply an API key. Their handling of uploaded images is governed by TypeSafe's own terms and privacy policy. You control the key and can clear it anytime in Settings.

Optional one-time support via Buy Me a Coffee opens in your browser and is not required to use Standing.

Permissions

  • Camera — posture checks (you can deny; tracking won't work).
  • Notifications — sit/stand nudges (optional).
  • Network — only to call TypeSafe when a check runs with your key.

Children

Standing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
